Click the screen first — that starts the sound. Arrow keys move, Space fires, B or M is the mega crash, fire + B loops. Running as an MSX turboR, but WebMSX does not emulate the turboR PCM: the sampled snare and the voices stay silent here (PSG and FM play). To hear them, use a real turboR, or openMSX with the system ROMs dumped from your own turboR. Emulation by WebMSX. Game source and ROM downloads: GitHub.
